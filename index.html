What it checks

Duplicate products
The same item listed twice under slightly different names, or with the same barcode. Sizes, colours, refills and multi-packs are recognised as different products, so they aren't flagged.
Repeated SKUs and barcodes
The same stock code or barcode on more than one row, which breaks inventory and imports.
Barcodes damaged by a spreadsheet
Excel turns long barcodes into "1.23457E+11", drops leading zeros and adds ".0". It also checks each barcode's check digit.
Prices
Currency symbols, decimal commas, thousands separators, text, zero or missing prices, and compare-at prices lower than the price.
Spelling and spacing
A brand or product type spelled two ways ("Acme" and "ACME"), extra spaces and invisible characters copied from websites.
